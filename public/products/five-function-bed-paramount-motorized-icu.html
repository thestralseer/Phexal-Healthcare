Product Description

The Five Function Bed (Paramount Motorized ICU) delivers clinical versatility for critical care units. Features 4 ultra-smooth LINAK actuators, dual-sided emergency CPR release levers, integrated angle indicators on split side rails, and central locking 150mm casters.
