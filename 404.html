This policy governs data processed by Phexal Healthcare Private Limited (GSTIN: 09AAHCP7621K1ZM, CDSCO Lic. UP/GHA/MD42/2026/000142), headquartered at 762, Makanpur, Nyay Khand I, Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh - 201014. 2 Information We Collect (B2B Institutional Only)

We collect organizational and administrative details strictly necessary to execute commercial medical device supply, customs clearance, and bedside clinical dispatch:

  • Authorized institutional procurement officer name, designation, and hospital/clinic name.
  • Corporate email address, direct phone contact, and dispatch delivery coordinates.
  • GSTIN, PAN, and billing data for statutory GST e-invoicing under Indian tax regulations.
  • Technical specifications, requested voltage calibrations, and purchase order quantities.

3 Zero Patient Telemetry & Health Records Non-Retention

Crucial Clinical Guarantee: Phexal Healthcare manufactures and distributes standalone biomedical hardware (ventilators, multipara monitors, infusion pumps, defibs). Our machines operate strictly under hospital intranet or autonomous offline clinical protocols. We do NOT monitor, intercept, store, record, transmit, or retain patient vitals, ECG waveforms, clinical diagnostic histories, or identifiable healthcare telemetry. Patient privacy remains 100% governed by the respective medical facility.

4 Strict Commercial Non-Disclosure

We do NOT rent, sell, trade, or monetize institutional procurement details or proforma quotation records with third-party ad exchanges, data brokers, or marketing syndicates. Data is shared exclusively with verified customs clearance brokers, maritime shipping carriers (for export CIF/FOB consignments), and statutory Indian tax portals as mandated by law.

5 Data Protection & 256-Bit SSL Architecture

All communications via our portal are secured with TLS 1.3/256-bit encryption. Physical warehouse servers and calibration ledgers are restricted through biometric two-factor controls, safeguarding client specifications from unauthorized intrusion.

6 Data Principal Rights & Statutory Grievance Redressal

In accordance with Section 11 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, institutional clients retain the right to review, update, or request permanent deletion of their commercial records upon fulfillment of warranty and statutory audit retention periods.
