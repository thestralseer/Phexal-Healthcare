3 Zero Patient Telemetry & Health Records Non-Retention

Crucial Clinical Guarantee: Phexal Healthcare manufactures and distributes standalone biomedical hardware (ventilators, multipara monitors, infusion pumps, defibs). Our machines operate strictly under hospital intranet or autonomous offline clinical protocols. We do NOT monitor, intercept, store, record, transmit, or retain patient vitals, ECG waveforms, clinical diagnostic histories, or identifiable healthcare telemetry. Patient privacy remains 100% governed by the respective medical facility.

4 Strict Commercial Non-Disclosure

We do NOT rent, sell, trade, or monetize institutional procurement details or proforma quotation records with third-party ad exchanges, data brokers, or marketing syndicates. Data is shared exclusively with verified customs clearance brokers, maritime shipping carriers (for export CIF/FOB consignments), and statutory Indian tax portals as mandated by law.

5 Data Protection & 256-Bit SSL Architecture

All communications via our portal are secured with TLS 1.3/256-bit encryption. Physical warehouse servers and calibration ledgers are restricted through biometric two-factor controls, safeguarding client specifications from unauthorized intrusion.

6 Data Principal Rights & Statutory Grievance Redressal

In accordance with Section 11 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, institutional clients retain the right to review, update, or request permanent deletion of their commercial records upon fulfillment of warranty and statutory audit retention periods.

Statutory Commercial Terms of Medical Trade

Official framework governing B2B institutional equipment sales, global export shipments under Incoterms 2020, and 24/7 emergency ICU rental dispatches.

1 CDSCO Licensing & Product Regulatory Clearance

All diagnostic and therapeutic equipment supplied by Phexal Healthcare complies with Medical Device Rules 2017 under valid CDSCO Form MD-42 licensing. Hardware holds ISO 13485:2016 quality management certification, accompanied by batch calibration certificates and serial number inspection logs.

2 Proforma Quotations & Institutional Pricing

Wholesale quotations and proforma tax invoices are issued with 15 calendar days validity. Bulk institutional pricing and turnkey hospital ICU outfitting are subject to mutually signed purchase contracts and staggered delivery schedules.

3 Global Maritime & Air Freight Export (FOB / CIF / CFR)

Consignments dispatched via JNPT/Mundra sea ports or Delhi IGI Air Cargo Terminal undergo pre-shipment metrology inspection, double-wall vacuum barrier crating, and marine insurance staging. All export customs documents (Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Form E/COO) are surrendered upon release.

4 Delhi NCR Emergency 24/7 ICU Rental SLA

Hospital and home ICU rental equipment is dispatched within 120 minutes across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, and Gurgaon. Units arrive in sanitized hermetic wrapping. A qualified biomedical technician conducts electrical safety tests and patient circuit calibration prior to handover.

5 Warranty, Calibration & Returns

All wholesale equipment includes 12 to 36 months comprehensive manufacturer warranty. Any damage during transit must be noted on the carrier consignment note within 24 hours of delivery. Calibration recalls or component exchanges are serviced directly by our biomedical engineering team.
