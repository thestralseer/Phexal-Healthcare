## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the clinical authority, surgical rigor, and supply-chain dependability of an enterprise medical equipment manufacturer and international exporter. Tailored for hospital procurement heads, biomedical engineers, international distributors, and intensive care specialists, the visual language prioritizes clinical precision, zero-ambiguity data communication, and instant high-trust compliance assurance.

Drawing from modern Corporate Precision and Technical Utilitarianism, the interface relies on crisp micro-borders, sterile neutral layering, and purposeful chromatic accents. Every surface mirrors the hygienic purity of a modern surgical suite or medical device cleanroom. 

Key tenets:
- **Institutional Authority:** Visuals project institutional reliability, institutional certifications (CE, ISO 13485, US FDA approval badges), and industrial engineering integrity.
- **Inquiry-Driven Velocity:** In alignment with enterprise B2B export and emergency rental workflows, pricing is omitted; interface actions channel decisively into Request for Quotation (RFQ), bulk freight proforma generation, and instant 24/7 ICU emergency rental procurement via direct dispatch desks.
- **Dual Operational Clarity:** Strict visual partitioning demarcates Global Bulk Export flows from regional Emergency ICU Equipment Rental desks.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical sterility with authoritative enterprise depth. 

- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate Navy):** Represents unyielding corporate gravity, governance, and structural permanence. Used for dominant headings, high-level structural containers, and primary technical headers.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9` - Clinical Bio-Teal):** The operational accent denoting digital precision, diagnostic technology, and product focus. Used for interactive states, spec highlights, active filter indicators, and high-priority RFQ triggers.
- **Tertiary / Action Accent (`#10B981` / `#059669` - Vital Emerald):** Represents uptime, 24/7 readiness, dispatch speed, and direct real-time communication. Strictly reserved for 24/7 ICU rental dispatch triggers, active equipment status chips, and WhatsApp instant order routing.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:** Base backgrounds utilize crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) paired with cool sterile slates (`#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9`) to define clear visual planes without heavy visual friction.
- **Borders & Partitions (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`):** Precise mechanical containment lines that frame technical specifications, data tables, and compliance modules.
- **Typography Neutrals:** High-legibility hierarchy using dark slate (`#0F172A`) for primary readability, `#334155` for descriptive documentation, and `#64748B` for meta-labels and technical annotations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the modern executive clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans for branding and display anchors with the systematic, high-density legibility of Inter for body text, technical datasheets, and regulatory tables.

- **Tabular Figures & Metrics:** All technical tables, parametric specifications (e.g., flow rates, battery back-up hours, dimensions, electrical tolerances), and ISO document references must enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure vertical column alignment across comparative matrices.
- **Specification Codes (`spec-code`):** Rendered in uppercase with widened letter spacing to ensure flawless human readability on clinical inspection checklists and shipment manifests.
- **Contextual Roles:** Headlines maintain a commanding structural presence with tight negative letter-spacing, projecting international corporate scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a structured 12-column desktop grid (max-width `1440px`) configured for high-density information architecture. It transitions to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).

- **Vertical Cadence:** A strict 8pt base grid coordinates all vertical margins and stack flows. Spacing tokens strictly demarcate relational proximity: sub-elements inside specification rows use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, component interiors use `space-md` and `space-lg`, and discrete clinical divisions use `space-xl`.
- **Layout Partitions:** The dual business division is managed via structural split shells:
  - **Global Export:** Broad, dense catalog arrays with multi-faceted filtering (certifications, freight packaging, container load volume).
  - **Regional ICU Desk:** Sticky utility docks, urgent availability indicators, and prominent geolocation targeting (Delhi NCR dispatch radius).
- **Responsive Handling:** Desktop interfaces emphasize side-by-side spec comparison and interactive RFQ trays. Mobile interfaces dock primary RFQ and WhatsApp actions to a persistent bottom clinical utility bar.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids decorative or heavy dropped shadows. Depth is achieved via sterile tonal layering and crisp mechanical outlines:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or sterile muted slate (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Pure white cards framed by a structural border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Subtle ambient drop on hover: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays / Drawers / Quick RFQ Modals):** Crisp white surface with an ambient clinical elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a precise 1px outline in `#CBD5E1`.
- **Emergency Priority Overlays (24/7 ICU Rental Desk):** Containers receive a subtle inset indicator line or high-contrast border in `#10B981` to communicate critical care availability.
- **Backdrop Treatments:** Modals and dynamic inquiry drawers employ an ultra-clean semi-opaque sterile veil: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with a `4px` backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, architectural shape language (Level 1: Soft). Medical equipment specifications demand precision and order; excessively round geometries compromise technical credibility.

- **Standard Components:** Buttons, inputs, specification containers, and technical cards use a `4px` radius (`0.25rem`).
- **Structural Groupings & Modals:** Large dashboard cards and inquiry sheets utilize an `8px` radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`).
- **Pills & Status Badges:** Technical compliance badges (e.g., "ISO 13485", "US FDA 510(k)", "CE Mark Class IIb") and device status chips (e.g., "Available for Delhi NCR Dispatch") use a calibrated pill shape (`9999px`) to immediately separate metadata from actionable structural controls.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary RFQ Button:** Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) solid background, pure white text, 4px corner radius, bold Inter label. Focus rings utilize `#0EA5E9` with an offset.
- **WhatsApp Direct Inquiry Button:** Vital Emerald (`#10B981`) background, white text, integrated official icon, with clear subtext indicating current dispatch status (e.g., "Delhi NCR 24/7 ICU Desk"). Hover shifts to `#059669`.
- **Secondary / Spec Sheet Download:** Crisp white surface, 1px outline (`#CBD5E1`), Navy text (`#0F172A`), with leading technical PDF or technical document glyphs.

### 2. Technical Specification Pills & ISO Badges
- **Compliance Badges:** Muted slate surface (`#F1F5F9`), slate navy border (`#CBD5E1`), dark navy text (`#0F172A`), `label-sm` font, uppercase tracking.
- **Rental Availability Badges:** Emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), emerald border (`#A7F3D0`), dark emerald text (`#065F46`), accompanied by a pulsing 6px green circular status beacon.

### 3. Equipment Cards (B2B Showcase)
- **Container:** Pure white card, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, 4px border radius.
- **Visuals:** Isolated, high-fidelity medical hardware photography on clean `#F8FAFC` backgrounds without busy lifestyle contexts.
- **Meta Area:** Technical model number in `spec-code`, equipment name in `headline-sm`, followed by a 2-column key specifications matrix (power rating, certifications, operational modes).
- **Price Treatment:** No prices are displayed anywhere. The card footer is anchored strictly by dual contextual actions: "Add to RFQ Matrix" and "Direct WhatsApp Spec Inquiry".

### 4. Input Fields & RFQ Forms
- **Field Styling:** Height of 44px, 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` background, `body-md` typography.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#0EA5E9` with a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`).
- **Specialized Inputs:** Integrated country-code telephone inputs, institution type dropdowns (Government Hospital, Private Healthcare Chain, Biomedical Importer, Emergency Homecare), and bulk container quantity steppers.

### 5. Division Navigation Switcher
- A persistent dual-division segmented toggle at the top navigation bar:
  - **Division A:** "Global Wholesale & Export" (accented with `#0EA5E9`).
  - **Division B:** "Delhi NCR 24/7 ICU Rental Desk" (accented with `#10B981` and active emergency dispatch indicator).

### 6. Lists, Comparative Spec Tables & RFQ Trays
- **Data Tables:** Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`. Dense cell padding (`8px 16px`), borders in `#E2E8F0`. Tabular figures aligned right; parameters aligned left.
- **Floating RFQ Tray:** A low-docked bar appearing upon item selection, summarizing selected SKUs, container cubic meter (CBM) estimates, and an instant proforma quote trigger.
